# 30-Day Action Plan for Increasing Website Visitors via Google Search

## Week 1: Foundation & Technical Setup
- [ ] **Google Tools:** Connect your website to **Google Search Console (GSC)** and **Google Analytics (GA4)**.
- [ ] **SEO Package:** Install an SEO package (like `artesaos/seotools` or `spatie/laravel-seo` for Laravel) to easily manage meta tags from the backend.
- [ ] **Sitemap:** Generate a `sitemap.xml` and submit it to Google Search Console. This tells Google exactly which pages exist on your site.
- [ ] **Robots.txt:** Create a `robots.txt` file in your `public/` directory to allow search engines to crawl your site.
- [ ] **Frontend Rendering:** Implement SSR (Server-Side Rendering) or pre-rendering for your Vue.js frontend to ensure Google can easily read your HTML without executing Javascript.

## Week 2: On-Page Optimization
- [ ] **Keyword Research:** Identify top 10 target keywords (e.g., using Ahrefs Free Keyword Generator, Ubersuggest, or Google Keyword Planner).
- [ ] **Meta Tags:** Update `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` tags on your homepage and all main feature pages to include your primary keywords.
- [ ] **Schema Markup:** Add `SoftwareApplication` Schema Markup to your homepage (see the SEO Master Guide for the code snippet).
- [ ] **Image Optimization:** Optimize all images (convert to `.webp` format, compress them, and add descriptive `alt` text).

## Week 3: Content Creation & Programmatic Pages
- [ ] **Landing Pages:** Create 3-5 "Use Case" landing pages targeting specific audiences (e.g., `/use-case/marketing`, `/use-case/education`).
- [ ] **Blogging:** Publish 2 high-quality blog posts targeting long-tail keywords (e.g., "How to automate [specific task] using AI in 2024").
- [ ] **Comparisons:** Add a comparison page (e.g., "YourTool vs ChatGPT" or "Best [Tool] Alternatives") as these have high purchase intent.

## Week 4: Link Building & Launch
- [ ] **AI Directories:** Submit your AI tool to at least 10 AI tool directories (Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, AI Valley, etc.). These provide strong backlinks and direct traffic.
- [ ] **Product Hunt:** Prepare for and execute a Product Hunt launch.
- [ ] **Social Seeding:** Share your tool on Reddit (r/SideProject, r/InternetIsBeautiful, r/OpenAI) and Twitter/X with a short, engaging demo video.
- [ ] **Review & Refine:** Review Google Search Console to see what search queries you are starting to rank for, and optimize your pages further based on that data.
